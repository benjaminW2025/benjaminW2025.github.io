A Survey of Benjamin Wang

Benjamin Wang1 — benjaminwang2025@gmail.com

Abstract

We present a longitudinal case study of an undergraduate at Dartmouth College studying mathematics and computer science. The subject exhibits a strong prior toward machine learning and interpretability research, with recurring excursions into pure math and optimization theory. Observed behaviors include reading papers for fun, writing them up on a personal blog, and occasionally proving small lemmas that turn out to already be known.

Outside of research, the subject is reliably found bouldering, logging meals on Beli, or outdoors in some capacity. No definitive explanation has been found for why these activities are pursued with the same intensity as research problems, though we suspect they are simply the same underlying drive pointed at different targets.

We conclude that further longitudinal study is warranted. Correspondence regarding collaboration, questions, or corrections to this manuscript is welcome via email.
